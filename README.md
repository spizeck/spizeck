# Hi, I'm Chad

I'm a business owner and operator based on **Saba, Dutch Caribbean**.

I don't run a software company. I build software because running real businesses and working in a small community exposes plenty of problems that technology can solve better.

Most of what you'll find here started with a real problem: something inefficient, something that didn't exist, or something I thought could work better.

## What I work on

### 🏝️ Business operations

Tools and systems built around the day-to-day realities of operating businesses on a small Caribbean island.

### 🤿 Tourism & diving

I own and operate [Sea Saba](https://www.seasaba.com), a dive operation on Saba. A growing amount of our technology is built in-house around the way we actually operate.

### 🏛️ Community & public-service projects

Software for local problems where an off-the-shelf solution either doesn't exist or doesn't fit Saba particularly well.

### 🍺 Brewing & hospitality

I also work in brewing and hospitality, which creates an entirely different collection of operational problems worth solving.

## How I build

I'm not a career software developer.

My background is in operating businesses. I approach software the same way I approach operations: understand the problem, define the rules, build a system, test it against reality, and keep improving it.

AI has dramatically changed what's possible for someone working this way. I use AI coding agents extensively for implementation while I focus on product direction, domain knowledge, architecture, testing, review, and deciding what should actually be built.

The result is increasingly serious software built around problems I understand firsthand.

## The common thread

I like finding places where **real-world experience, good systems, and technology intersect**.

Sometimes that's a booking workflow. Sometimes it's water delivery. Sometimes it's a brewery, a dive boat, or a football pool.

If it can work better, I'm probably interested in figuring out how.
